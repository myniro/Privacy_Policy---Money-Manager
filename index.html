Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy for Money Manager & Expense Wallet

Application: Money Manager & Expense Wallet Package: com.myniro.moneymanager.zk9v Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Strict Local-First Privacy Policy: Money Manager & Expense Wallet is an offline-first personal financial manager. All your transactions, expense entries, account balances, budgets, memos, and receipt photos remain strictly on your device. We do not operate external developer servers, we do not require account registration, and we never collect, view, or sell your personal financial records.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile application Money Manager & Expense Wallet (the "App"), developed for Android devices and published on Google Play. This document clearly discloses the data practices of the App in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

2. Data Handled by the App (On-Device Storage Only)

2.1 Financial & Transaction Information

The App allows you to record personal finances. The following information is stored exclusively in your device's local database (Room / SQLite):

  • Transaction amounts, dates, and times
  • Expense and income categories (e.g., Food, Shopping, Transport, Salary)
  • Wallet and account names (e.g., Cash, Bank, Credit Card)
  • Optional transaction notes and descriptions
  • Monthly budget thresholds and targets

Storage & Access: This information is stored solely within the App's private internal storage sandbox on your Android device. It is never transmitted across the network to our servers or any unauthorized third party.

2.2 Receipt Photos & Profile Images

When you attach a photo of a receipt or choose an avatar image:

  • The App uses the secure Android Photo Picker or system file selector.
  • The selected image is saved locally in the App's private file directory.
  • Images are never uploaded to any remote server and can be deleted by you at any time.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may process:

  • Advertising ID (AAID): To serve relevant ads and limit ad repetition in accordance with Google Play Advertising ID policy.
  • Device Information & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, performance diagnostics, and crash logs to ensure ad delivery.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

3.2 In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers an optional one-time Lifetime VIP upgrade to remove advertisements:

  • All purchase transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing.
  • The developer does not collect, process, or store payment card details, bank credentials, or billing addresses.

4. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its intended functionality. We strictly adhere to Android least-privilege principles:

4.1 Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

On Android 13 (API level 33) and higher, the App requests runtime permission to post notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to deliver your scheduled personal expense tracking reminders (e.g., daily evening reminders so you never forget to log your daily spendings) and budget threshold alerts.
  • Local-Only Trigger: All reminders and notifications are scheduled and triggered entirely locally on your device via Android's local alarm and notification managers. No remote push server, notification tracking service, or external network calls are involved.
  • User Control: This permission is completely optional. You can enable, disable, change reminder times, or revoke notification permissions at any time directly within the App's Settings or through your device's system settings (Settings > Apps > Money Manager > Notifications).

4.2 Other Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely by Google AdMob (to deliver ads in the free version) and Google Play Billing (to verify one-time Lifetime VIP purchase status). The App does not transmit your personal transactions over the internet.

6. User Data Control and Deletion

Because all personal financial data is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleting Entries: You can edit or delete individual transactions, budgets, or wallets at any time within the App.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can wipe all app data by clearing storage via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Money Manager > Storage > Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the App. This immediately and permanently erases all databases and receipts from your phone.

7. Children's Privacy

Money Manager & Expense Wallet is designed for adult financial tracking and does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
